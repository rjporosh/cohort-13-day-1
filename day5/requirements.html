The Account Holder's Wants

01

What the user wants

Primary story

As an account holder, I want to view my bank statement for a chosen range of dates, so that I can review every transaction that happened in that period.

Opening & closing balance

As an account holder, I want to see my balance at the start and end of the period, so that I understand how my money moved.

Line-by-line detail

As an account holder, I want each entry to show its date, description, debit or credit, and the running balance, so that I can trace every change to my account.

This is mine

As an account holder, I want my name, account number and card shown on the statement, so that I can confirm the statement belongs to me.

Period totals

As an account holder, I want the total money in and out for the period, so that I can summarise my income and spending at a glance.

What "done" looks like — from the user's chair
  • I pick a start date and an end date, and I get exactly that window.
  • The header carries the bank name, my name, account number and card.
  • Every row is dated and described in plain language.
  • Money out is clearly separated from money in.
  • A running balance follows every entry, top to bottom.
  • Opening balance, closing balance and totals all reconcile.
