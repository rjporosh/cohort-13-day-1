
Human Resource Management Policy

1. Annual Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 20 days of annual leave per year.
Employees must obtain manager approval before taking leave.
Unused annual leave can be carried forward for up to 5 days.

2. Sick Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 10 days of sick leave per year.
A medical certificate may be required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days.
Employees must notify their manager when taking sick leave.

3. Work From Home Policy

Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week.
Employees must obtain approval from their department manager.
All employees must remain available during regular office hours.

4. Employee Performance Policy

Employee performance is evaluated twice a year.
Performance evaluation considers productivity, teamwork, and technical skills.
Managers are responsible for providing feedback to employees.
